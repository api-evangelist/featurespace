# Featurespace

> Featurespace is an enterprise financial-crime prevention company, founded in 2008 out of the University of Cambridge and acquired by Visa (announced September 2024). Its flagship ARIC Risk Hub uses Adaptive Behavioral Analytics and Automated Deep Behavioral Networks to risk-score events in real time for fraud, scam, and anti-money-laundering (AML) detection across banks, payment processors, acquirers, insurers, and gaming operators in 180+ countries.

Featurespace does not publish a public, unauthenticated developer API. Product and developer documentation (docs.featurespace.com) sits behind a customer login, and the ARIC mobile SDKs are distributed under access request rather than public registries.
